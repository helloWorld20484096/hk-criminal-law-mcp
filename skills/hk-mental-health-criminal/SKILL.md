# HK Mental Health and Criminal Law

> **Not legal advice.** Mental-health disposals engage liberty, consent, and
> medical ethics. Instruct a suitably qualified expert and verify the current
> statutory framework.

## Step 1 — Identify the issue

- Fitness/ability to participate in the proceedings.
- Fitness to plead (if raised).
- Mental state at the time of the alleged offence.
- Diminished responsibility (for homicide — verify the current statutory
  provision and common-law position).
- Insanity / automatism.
- Vulnerability in custody or interview.
- Need for a hospital order or psychiatric disposal.
- Risk of self-harm or suicide.

## Step 2 — Obtain the legal framework

Retrieve the Mental Health Ordinance (Cap. 136) provisions relevant to:
- definitions of mental disorder;
- admission, detention, and treatment;
- hospital orders and guardianship;
- reports and medical recommendations;
- transfer of prisoners;
- leave and discharge.

Also retrieve the criminal procedure provisions on fitness and disposal
(Cap. 221), and any relevant sentencing provisions.

## Step 3 — Obtain expert evidence

Instruct a psychiatrist/psychologist with the right expertise. The report
should address:
- diagnosis and classification;
- symptoms and their effect on thinking, perception, and behaviour;
- cognitive capacity and suggestibility;
- fitness to participate/plead;
- mental state at the time of the offence;
- risk of violence, self-harm, and suicide;
- treatment needs and available facilities;
- recommendations for disposal.

Provide the expert with the relevant materials and the legal questions. Do not
ask the expert to decide the legal issue.

## Step 4 — Procedural safeguards

- Ensure the client understands the proceedings and can give instructions.
- Consider an interpreter, intermediary, or communication support.
- Consider whether a litigation friend/guardian is needed.
- Record any consent issues for treatment and disclosure.
- Check whether the client is on remand, in hospital, or in custody; ensure
  continuity of medication and care.
- Consider bail conditions with treatment/supervision requirements.

## Step 5 — Disposal options

Verify availability and eligibility:
- hospital order with/without restrictions;
- guardianship order;
- probation with a mental-health condition;
- community treatment/supervision;
- prison with healthcare;
- transfer to hospital;
- absolute/conditional discharge where appropriate.

Weigh public protection, treatment needs, proportionality, and the least
restrictive option.

## Escalation

- Instruct experienced counsel and a forensic psychiatrist.
- If there is an immediate suicide/self-harm risk, prioritize medical and
  custodial safeguarding; do not delay for legal analysis.
- Consider a second expert opinion if the diagnosis or fitness conclusion is
  disputed.
